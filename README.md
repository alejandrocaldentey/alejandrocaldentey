I'm Alejandro Caldentey, a Data Analytics specialist based in Barcelona. Industrial engineer by training, data scientist by practice, business strategist by necessity: a combination rarely found together in someone who works with data. I operate independently under the Datalethia brand, designing and building decision systems for SME executives - systems that connect a business's scattered data and answer, without manual intervention, what's happening, why, and what to do next. Previously: data projects at Schneider Electric, Accenture, and Vibia.
